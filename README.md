## 💡 Game Overview

It's a quiet evening in your cozy study room... until an army of pesky mosquitoes descends upon your glowing lamp! Grab your classic red flyswatter and slap those annoying pests before they bite.

Be careful! A friendly **Honeybee 🐝** is peacefully passing by—swatting the innocent pollinator will result in a painful sting, screen flash, and costs 1 of your 3 hearts!

---

## ✨ Features & Gameplay Mechanics

- **🏮 Cozy Evening Atmosphere**:
  - Warm retro patterned wallpaper with ambient lighting and an overhead glowing reading lamp.
  - Desktop arcade cabinet container on PC with custom animated flyswatter cursor, expanding to fullscreen swatting on mobile.
- **🦟 Quirky Mosquito Cast**:
  - **Regular Pesky Mosquito 🦟** (+100 pts) — Common brown mosquito with rapid fluttering wings.
  - **Bloated Blood Mosquito 🩸** (+250 pts) — Slower, plump red belly with an extra juicy comic splat!
  - **Ninja Stealth Mosquito 🥷** (+400 pts) — Sleek dark mosquito with fast zig-zag evasion darts.
  - **Golden Queen Mosquito 🌟** (+500 pts) — Rare sparkling mosquito that bursts into shimmering stars.
- **🐝 Friendly Honeybee Hazard**:
  - Innocent striped bumblebee gently buzzing through the room.
  - Swatting the bee triggers an angry sting, screen shake, and costs 1 Heart (❤️).
- **💥 Splat Visuals & Particle FX**:
  - Meaty comic cartoon splat decals on the wall with cartoon dizzy stars and floating score counters.
  - Authentic flyswatter slap animation on every tap or click.
- **🔥 Combo Multiplier**:
  - Chain consecutive mosquito hits without missing or letting bites escape to reach up to `3.0x Swat Master` frenzy!
- **🔊 Procedural Web Audio Engine**:
  - Procedural FM synthesis buzzing (`bzzZZZ`) with Doppler pitch shifts as mosquitoes dart around.
  - Crisp meaty swatter slap impact, bee sting buzz-zap, squeak splats, and game over fanfares.
- **💾 Local High Score Tracking**:
  - Best score, highest combo, and swat accuracy stored in `localStorage`.

## 🕹️ Controls & Rules

| Input | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Tap / Left Click** | Swat with flyswatter |
| **Spacebar** | Pause / Resume game |
| **R** | Quick restart match |

- **❤️ 3 Hearts / Lives**: You start with 3 lives. Swatting a friendly bee or letting too many mosquitoes bite you will cost a life.
- **⚡ Reflex & Precision**: Build streaks with accurate swats to maximize points.
